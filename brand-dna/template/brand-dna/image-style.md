## 4. Real people

Real people, real events and real work are shown with real photos. The one exception is an AI image or video of one of us or our staff, and only after we've confirmed that person has agreed. It is labelled as AI-made wherever it's used, never presented as a real photo, and never shows a real customer, event or job. We never make AI images of our customers. Where a platform offers its own AI label, such as Meta's AI info label, we use it.
