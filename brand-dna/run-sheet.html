Brand DNA run sheet

The 90-minute session in timed blocks. What I say, what the client does, and which prompt runs. The prompts live on the Brand DNA page; every pill below links to one. The client drives their own Claude, so the folder and the wiring end up in their account.

The session

Ninety minutes, nine blocks

Times are from the start. The client has the Claude desktop app open on task work with the brand-dna folder chosen. I read the prompts out and they paste them, or they share their screen and I talk them through it.

0:004 min

Open

I say
Today we build one folder that every AI tool you use reads before it makes anything. Five parts: what you have already got, a short interview, the files, pointing your tools at them, and a test on real work. One rule runs through all of it. When a request and your brand disagree, the tool asks you which wins and writes your answer down. It never guesses.
Client

Opens Claude, task work, chooses the brand-dna folder.

Prompt

None.

0:046 min

What you have already got

I say
I read everything you sent. Here is where your own documents disagree with each other. Every one of these is a coin your AI tools have been tossing.
Client

Reads the conflicts in sources/audit-report.md, starting with the three I starred. Reacts out loud. No decisions typed yet: the interview captures them.

Prompt

None live. 1.1 ran yesterday.

0:1030 min

The interview

I say
It asks one question at a time. Answer out loud and type what you said, or I will type for you. Short and real beats polished. When it gets to the conflicts, you pick the winner. If you cannot pick, say so and it stays open.
Client

Answers up to twenty questions, starting with one list of plain facts: hours, who works there, contact details, handles, time for social media. Says "that's it" at the end.

Prompt
2.1 The interview

Close

Three things happen next. The AI image rules and a playbook for each channel, which you read and approve. Then the booklet, built from these files. Then a separate reviewer checks the folder and the booklet before anyone relies on them. All of it by [AGREED DATE]. From now on every change goes through one of three prompts: add a rule, change a rule, or the monthly check. Never edit a tool's instructions by hand.

Running behind

The interview is the block that overruns. Let it, and take the time from voice: run 4.1 and leave the client to read it after. Never cut the interview, the wiring or the test: the first is where the brand comes from, the second is the reason for the whole session, and the third is what the client remembers.

Why the AI image rules and the playbooks come after: a dry run of the whole pack on a sample business took two and a half to three hours with the owner present. Ninety minutes holds the parts that need the client in the room.
