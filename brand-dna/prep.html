Before your Brand DNA session

In the session we build one folder that every AI tool you use reads before it makes anything for you. Everything below is the raw material. The more of it you send, the more of the 90 minutes goes on building rather than asking.

What to send

Seven things, as they are

Do not tidy anything. Old, messy and contradictory is exactly what we are looking for, because the contradictions are what make your AI tools drift.

0 of 7 ready

Two quick lists

Where you post, and what makes things for you

  1. Your channels. Every place you post or send from, with your handle: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, email newsletter, website, Google Business Profile.
  2. Your AI tools. Every AI tool, project, custom assistant, scheduled task or automation that writes, designs or posts anything for you. If you are not sure whether something counts, list it.

How to send it

One shared folder, before the day

  1. Make one folder in Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive called brand-dna sources.
  2. Drop everything in. Screenshots, files, notes. File names do not matter.
  3. Share it with me and send me the link two working days before the session, so I can read it all before we start.

On the day

What to have open

  1. The Claude desktop app, signed in to your paid plan, on the computer you will use day to day. The build happens in your own account so your tools can read it afterwards.
  2. Access to your AI tools, so we can point each one at the new folder in the session.
  3. Ninety minutes, uninterrupted, and whoever else decides how your business sounds and looks.
