# Magnum AI: logo design brief

For a designer. Written 14 September 2026.

Everything in "The house you are joining" already exists and is in daily use.
It is not a mood board and it is not negotiable without a conversation. The
rest is open.

---

## 1. The business

Magnum AI is a one-person AI consultancy on the Gold Coast. James Wheable is
the owner, the consultant and the presenter. There is no team, and there is
not going to be one. That is a deliberate choice, not a stage.

The clients are small business owners: trades, allied health, retail,
professional services. People who run something real and want AI to do actual
work, not people who want to talk about AI. Most are over forty and read on a
phone.

The work is coaching, systems builds and done-for-you automation. Clients get
a members area of courses, prompts and reference guides at
`webes77.github.io/magnum-guides`, which is the best single look at the
current visual language.

**The register.** Expert peer, not service provider. Dry, direct, Australian,
never American. Nothing breathless, nothing that sounds like a technology
startup. The brand should look like it belongs to someone with twenty-five
years of experience who happens to work in AI, rather than to an AI company.

---

## 2. What is being designed

A logo system, not a single picture:

1. A mark that works on its own.
2. A wordmark for "Magnum AI".
3. Horizontal and stacked lockups of the two.
4. A reduced version of the mark for small sizes.
5. A one-colour version of everything.

---

## 3. The house you are joining

### Colour

| Role | Hex | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#FBFBF9` | The page ground |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on the paper |
| Navy | `#1F2A37` | Mastheads, footers, dark plates |
| Ink | `#1E1B17` | Body text, 2px borders |
| Coral | `#EF4029` | The one accent. Display type, fills, figures |
| Deep coral | `#C63A2A` | Any coral text under 18px |
| Bright coral | `#FF6F5E` | Coral on navy only, and badges |
| Coral tint | `#FBE1D8` | Tags and quiet panels |
| Slate | `#5B6B7A` | Ticks and figure detail |

Two coral values exist for a measured reason. `#EF4029` is 3.73:1 on paper,
which is fine for display type and fails for body text. `#C63A2A` is 5.02:1
and carries anything small. If the mark or the wordmark includes text under
18px, use the deep value.

Coral is the only accent. Do not add a second.

### Type

- Display: **Oswald**
- Body: **IBM Plex Sans**
- Labels, data and prompts: **IBM Plex Mono**

Do not introduce a fourth typeface. If the wordmark is drawn rather than set,
it should still look like it belongs beside Oswald.

### Retired, do not reintroduce

- Black and gold.
- The beige and rust system: paper `#EEE6D3`, rust `#B8452A`, olive `#6C7A3F`.
- Grid textures and offset shadows.
- Playfair, Bebas Neue, Archivo Narrow and Inter.

## 4. Where it has to work

This list is the brief. A mark that fails any line here is not finished.

1. A browser tab at **16 x 16 px**, and an app icon at **32 x 32 px**.
2. A share card at **1200 x 630 px**, sitting in a corner.
3. The masthead of a presentation deck, on navy, at roughly 40px tall.
4. An email footer, on white, at roughly 32px tall.
5. A4 print and PDF, in colour and in black only.
6. **One colour**, on a light ground and knocked out of a dark one.
7. Somebody else's invoice, slide or printed document, where you control
   nothing about the surrounding design.

Point 1 is the one that kills concepts. Any mark with more than about four
distinct elements turns to mush at 16px. Assume the full mark will not survive
it and that a reduction is needed. Say so rather than hiding it.

---

## 5. Where the thinking got to

A first direction was drafted in-house and rejected, and the reasons are more
useful than the drawing.

**The idea.** Magnum AI's method is the 6 Lever Framework: Role, Context,
Constraints, Tone, Format, Output. Those six labels are fixed and are never
renamed, reordered or added to. The members area already draws them as six
sliders at different settings, so the mark was six bars of different lengths
with the last in coral.

**What was right.** The levers are the intellectual property. A mark that
encodes them says something true, which beats initials in a square.

**What was wrong, and matters.**

- The lockup put "SIX LEVERS" under "MAGNUM AI" like a tagline. That demotes
  the business to a subtitle of its own method. **The business is called
  Magnum AI.** The levers are how the work is done. Do not let the framework
  become the identity.
- Horizontal bars read as a bar chart. If the sliders idea is used at all,
  it should read as an **equaliser**, which means vertical faders, not
  horizontal bars.
- Six of anything cannot hold 16px. See section 4.

**Open question for you, not a decision already made.** Whether the levers
belong in the mark at all. The counter-argument is that it welds the identity
to one framework, and if the framework ever stops being the centre of what
Magnum AI sells, the logo is wrong. A designer who thinks the whole direction
is a mistake should say so and show why.

---

## 6. What to avoid

- Initials in a square or a circle. It is what every consultancy does when
  nobody could think of anything.
- The AI visual clichés: circuit traces, brains, neural nodes, hexagon
  meshes, glowing orbs, purple-to-blue gradients.
- Anything that reads as an audio or music app, which is the live risk if the
  equaliser idea is taken literally.
- Gradients, bevels, drop shadows and glows. The house style is flat.
- A mark that only works in two colours.

---

## 7. Deliverables

1. Primary mark as **SVG**, plus the reduced small-size version.
2. Wordmark as SVG, with the type outlined.
3. Horizontal and stacked lockups as SVG.
4. One-colour versions of all of the above.
5. Favicon set: 16, 32, 48 and 180px PNG, plus an SVG favicon.
6. PNG exports at 1x and 2x for anything that ships as a raster.
7. A one-page usage note: which version at which size, minimum sizes, clear
   space, and what not to do.

Files must be usable by one person with no design software. Assume the owner
will be dropping SVGs into HTML by hand.

---

## 8. Process

Show a **range first**. Three to six genuinely different directions as rough
marks in black on white, before any refinement or colour. The owner is a fast,
decisive reader and would rather point at one of six than polish one of one.

Then one direction refined, with the 16px proof shown at real size in the
first round of refinement rather than the last.
