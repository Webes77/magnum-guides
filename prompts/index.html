How to use a card

Copy puts the exact prompt on your clipboard. Expand opens it full screen for reading. Anything in [square brackets] is yours to fill in before you send it.

The strip above each prompt shows which of the six levers it pulls on: Role, Context, Constraints, Tone, Format, Output. Read the strip, then read the prompt, and you can see the method doing its work before you use it.

The shelf is grouped by when you would run a card: starting a task, before you act on an answer, setting up, every week, every month. The row of chips narrows it further by type, or by a single lever. New cards land under New until they are filed. Every card keeps its link, so anything already shared still works.

No card matches all of that.
