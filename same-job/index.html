Same job, other tool

The courses use Claude. If your business runs on ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot, everything still works. Here is what your tool calls each thing, and where to find it.

Checked 1 Nov 2026

The short version

The prompts work anywhere. What changes between tools is the name of the setting, where it lives, and which plan has it.

Any card on the Prompt Shelf without a “Claude only” tag pastes straight into ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot and does the same job. It is like swapping spanner brands. The bolt doesn’t care.

Pick your tool below and the page shows only that one.

Remember past chats

It picks up what you told it last week. Check what it has kept now and then. Privacy guide

  • ChatGPTMemoryIn Settings, under Personalisation. Free plans remember recent chats only.

Your screen may read differently

These menus move every few weeks, and plans change what they include. If a name doesn’t match, search the tool’s settings for the job in plain words, like “instructions” or “memory”. Checked against the makers’ own pages and tech press on 1 Nov 2026, and re-checked every month.

What still needs Claude

A few things on the members area lean on Claude features the others don’t match yet. They carry a “Claude only” tag on the Prompt Shelf.

  • Working through a folder on your computer. Sorting, filing and auditing files where they sit.
  • The agent workforce. A team of agents built in Claude Code.

None of that stops you learning on the tool you have. Start with the prompts, and move only when you hit one of these.
