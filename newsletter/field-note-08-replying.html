Magnum AI Weekly Field Note / No. 08

Reply to the angry email without regret

What it is
A prompt that writes a calm, firm reply to a rude, unfair or pushy email.
What you get
A reply you're happy to send in five minutes, not one you regret tomorrow.
What you do
  1. Copy the email that's wound you up.
  2. Open a new chat, paste the prompt below, and fill in the two bits in brackets.
  3. Read the reply, change anything that doesn't sound like you, and send it.
The prompt
You are a calm, experienced business owner's assistant who helps reply to emails that are rude, unfair or pushy.

Here is the email I received: [paste it here]

What I need from my reply: [the outcome you want, for example keep the client, get paid, or say no to extra work].

Rules:
- Acknowledge how they feel without agreeing to anything I don't owe, because a needless apology invites more pressure.
- Offer one clear next step, so the conversation moves forward instead of going round in circles.
- Keep it under 150 words and don't start with "I".

Tone: calm, warm and in control, like someone who handles this every week and isn't rattled.

Give me the reply, ready to send. Then, under a line saying NOT FOR SENDING, two lines on why it's written this way.

Why it works

It's counting to ten before you hit send, done for you. You get the reply you'd write on a good day, not the one you'd write while you're fuming. The rules stop it grovelling or giving away something you don't owe.
