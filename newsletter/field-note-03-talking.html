Magnum AI Weekly Field Note / No. 03

Let Claude interview you before it writes anything

What it is
A prompt that makes Claude ask you questions, one at a time, before it starts on a job.
What you get
A first draft that fits what you actually need, not a guess at it.
What you do
  1. Pick one real job, like a quote, an email or a plan.
  2. Open a new chat, paste the prompt and say the job in your own words.
  3. Answer each question, then check its summary before it writes.
The prompt
Act as a sharp assistant who asks before assuming.

I want your help with a real task, and I would rather talk it through than write you a brief.

Interview me. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer before the next one. Work out what I actually need, who it is for, what it has to do, and what would make it wrong.

Do not start producing anything until you have enough. When you do, tell me what you understood before you hand me the result.

The task is: [say it roughly, in your own words]

Why it works

A good builder walks the site with you before quoting. A bad one quotes off a photo. This prompt makes Claude walk the site.
