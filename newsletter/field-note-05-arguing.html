Magnum AI Weekly Field Note / No. 05

Get Claude to find the hole in your plan

What it is
A prompt that makes Claude argue against your plan instead of agreeing with it.
What you get
The one problem that would stop it working, found before you spend the money.
What you do
  1. Fill in the two boxes: your business, then your plan in a few lines.
  2. Copy the prompt into a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT.
  3. Fix the one that matters before anything else.
The prompt
You are the most experienced operator in my industry, brought in to find the reason this plan fails. Nobody is paying you to be encouraging.

The plan below is mine and I am about to spend my own time and money on it, so a miss here costs me a week of work, not a mark out of ten.

Work only from what is written. Where the plan leans on something I have not told you, say so rather than filling the gap, because an invented risk sends me chasing a problem I do not have.

Be blunt and specific. Name the thing, not the category.

Give it back under three headings.

THE ONE THAT MATTERS
The single objection that would actually stop this working, and what it costs me if I act before fixing it.

WHAT I HAVE ASSUMED
Each assumption the plan rests on, with the question I should have asked to test it.

WHAT YOU WOULD WANT TO KNOW
The things you would ask before you put your own name on this.

If the plan holds up, say so in one line. Do not invent a fourth problem to look useful.

MY BUSINESS
[what you sell, who to, where, how many staff]

MY PLAN
[your plan, a few lines is plenty]

Why it works

Ask a mate if your idea is good and he'll say yes. Ask a bank manager and you'll hear what's wrong with it. This prompt makes Claude the bank manager.
