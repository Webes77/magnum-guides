Magnum AI Weekly Field Note / No. 07

Check your bill with Claude before you renew

What it is
A prompt that reads your actual bill or contract and tells you whether to renew, switch or cancel.
What you get
An answer about your prices and your terms, not general advice about plans like yours.
What you do
  1. Save the bill, contract or renewal notice, or take a screenshot of it.
  2. Open a new chat, attach the file and paste the prompt below.
  3. If it says something is missing, find it and send it back.
The prompt
Act as a careful adviser who reads the fine print before answering.

I'm deciding whether to renew, switch or cancel the plan in the attached document.

Work only from what's in the document. If something I need isn't there, like the end date, the exit fee or the price after a discount ends, tell me what to find. Don't guess, because a guessed figure looks exactly like a real one.

Keep it plain and short.

Give me two lists:
1. My options, one line each, with the real price or term from the document.
2. What you would do in my shoes, and the figures that decide it.

If the document doesn't change the usual advice, say so in one line.

Why it works

Ask a mechanic what's wrong before he's opened the bonnet and he'll still give you an answer. It'll be about cars like yours. Open the bonnet and it's about your car. Attaching the bill is opening the bonnet.
