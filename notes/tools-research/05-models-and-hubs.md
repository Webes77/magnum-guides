# 05 · One-stop image and video hubs, Fal.ai, Midjourney, and the models themselves, October 2026

Researched 11 October 2026 by web search only (WebSearch, extended mode for every price and every change inside the last three months). This file builds on `03-images-video.md` and does not repeat it: Gemini, Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images 2.5, Ideogram 4.5, Seedance 2.5, Higgsfield and Kling are covered there and only updated here where something new turned up. Direct fetch was tried on krea.ai and fal.ai and both returned ENOTFOUND, the same as every vendor page in file 03, so every figure below comes from search-result snippets. Where a snippet quotes the vendor's own current page it is graded A with "via snippet"; otherwise B or C.

Grades: A = vendor's own current page (here read via search snippet, not fetched). B = reputable press. C = blog, review or comparison site, reseller, affiliate page, forum, user review site, or a leaderboard aggregator that is not the leaderboard itself. Dates are the source's publication or "checked" date where the snippet showed one; "seen 11 Oct" means the page was live in results that day with no date visible.

Currency convention: same as file 03. Every price says AUD or USD and whether tax is in. USD tools billed to an Australian card usually add 10% GST at checkout, plus a possible foreign transaction fee of up to about 3%. AUD estimates assume about A$1.55 per US$1 and add GST, so "about A$X with GST" means USD x 1.55 x 1.1. They are estimates, not quotes.

The two things to know before reading on. First, Freepik is now called Magnific (since 28 April 2026); any client who says "Freepik" means this product. Second, the hubs disagree with each other and with themselves about prices more than any category researched so far, and most of them sell by credits that do not map cleanly to "a picture" or "a second of video". Every "how many" below is an estimate from a source, not a promise.

## 1. One-stop shops (beyond Fal.ai and Higgsfield)

A hub is one login and one bill that reaches other companies' models: Google's, OpenAI's, ByteDance's, Kuaishou's and so on. What the owner gets is choice without five subscriptions. What they pay for it is a credit system, a markup on each model, and a second company between them and the model maker.

Shortlisted (four strongest for this reader): Krea, Magnific (formerly Freepik), Adobe Firefly, OpenArt. Dropped, with reasons, at 1.5.

### 1.1 Krea

**What it does, plainly.** A browser app where you type a prompt, pick a model from a menu, and get images or video. Also has its own image model (Krea 2), a real-time canvas, upscaling and a node workflow tool.

**Models now.** Image: Krea 2 (its own, launched 12 May 2026), Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2 Lite, GPT Image 2, Flux family (A via snippet, Krea model pages, seen 11 Oct). FLUX 3 is listed as "Soon" on a Krea page that may be stale (A via snippet). Video: Seedance 2.5, promoted as new on Krea's home page (A via snippet); Veo 3.1, Kling (Kling O1 and 3.0 named), Hailuo 3, Wan 3.0, Runway Gen-4 and LTX-2 per one roundup (C). Not found on Krea: GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2.1, Kling 4.0 (only third-party pages claim them). Several third-party pages still say Pro includes Sora, which closed in April and September 2026; ignore that line.

**Price (USD, tax not stated).** Most recent third-party checks agree on Basic US$9/mo with 5,000 compute units, Pro US$35/mo with 20,000, Max US$105/mo with 60,000 (C, several, to Sep 2026). Older pages give Max as US$70 with 40,000 (C). Business about US$200/mo (C, single source). Annual discount reported between 20% and 40% (C, conflicting).
- What Pro buys, from third-party estimates only: about 257 Nano Banana 2 images (about US$0.14 each), or about 83 Seedance 2.0 clips, or 19 to 27 Veo 3 clips (C, two sources disagree on Veo). Krea's own blog says Veo 3.1 is billed at a fixed price per clip, citing US$1.26 for a six-second silent clip, rather than from plan units (A via snippet, Krea blog). Veo 3.1 and Kling 3.0 unit costs: UNSETTLED.
- Video needs Pro. Basic covers image and 3D models and only "selected" video models (C, several agree).
- AUD estimate: Basic about A$15/mo with GST, Pro about A$60/mo with GST.

**Free tier.** 100 compute units a day, no card, limited models (C, several). Free output is not licensed for commercial use; the commercial licence starts at Basic (C, several, including a Krea-authored blog). Watermarking on free: one source says yes, another does not mention it (C, UNSETTLED). Usable for an owner to try the interface, not for anything published.

**Commercial use.** Paid plans include a commercial licence (C, Krea blog and comparison sites agree).

**Claude.** Yes. Krea's own MCP page says add it in Claude's connector settings or with one Claude Code command; server `https://api.krea.ai/mcp`, OAuth sign-in, and OAuth use is billed to the compute units of the workspace you pick on the consent screen (A via snippet, krea.ai/mcp and Krea developer docs, seen 11 Oct). Whether a free Krea account can use it: UNSETTLED.

**Ease of use.** One comparison calls the interface minimal and accessible to beginners (C, thin evidence). The model menu is long, which is the real learning cost.

**Red flags.** Trustpilot complaints in December 2025 to March 2026: charged after deleting an account, unused units removed on cancellation, emails unanswered (C, individual reviews). Krea's own refund rule: a refund only if the workspace used under 650 compute units and under 20 generations that month, requested through Stripe's customer portal; plans auto-renew until cancelled (A via snippet, Krea docs refunds page). That is strict. Start on monthly.

**Proposed verdict.** Only if the owner wants one cheap login to try many image models (Basic, about A$15/mo, is the cheapest commercial licence of the hubs) or wants video from several makers on one bill (Pro, about A$60/mo). Strict refunds.

Sources: krea.ai/mcp, krea.ai/docs developers MCP page, krea.ai/docs refunds-and-billing, krea.ai model pages and blog "Seedance 2.5 vs Veo 3.1" (A via snippet, seen 11 Oct); costbench.com, piclumen.com, photta.app, reviewner.com, comparedge.com, tooljunction.io, comparateur-ia.com (C, Apr to Sep 2026); trustpilot.com krea.ai regional pages (C, Dec 2025 to Mar 2026); prnewswire.com fal x Krea 2 (C, 27 May 2026); theplanettools.ai, rits.shanghai.nyu.edu on Krea 2 (C, May to Jun 2026).

### 1.2 Magnific (formerly Freepik)

**What it does, plainly.** The old Freepik stock site plus an AI suite: image and video generation from many makers, upscaling, editing, and the stock library, under one subscription. Freepik renamed itself Magnific on 28 April 2026; accounts and subscriptions carried over (B, TNW; A, PR Newswire release). Reported US$230M in annual recurring revenue and never externally funded (company figures, not audited).

**Models now.** Image: GPT Image 2.5 in both its Flare and Sunburst variants (A via snippet, Magnific image-models docs), Nano Banana 2 and Nano Banana Pro (A via snippet, pricing page), Seedream 5.0 Pro and Flux.2 Pro (C), Nano Banana 2.1 claimed added on 6 Oct in a sponsored post only (C, UNSETTLED). Its own Magnific One, launched 8 October 2026, which plans the art direction before generating and has a Brand Kit that pulls your logo, colours and fonts from your website or a PDF and applies them (B, VentureBeat; A, PR Newswire). VentureBeat reports One runs on OpenAI's GPT Image 2 underneath; another outlet says Magnific trained no new model; Magnific presents it as its own (B and C, conflict noted). Video: Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1 (with Fast and Lite), Runway Gen-4.5, Seedance 2.0, Wan 3.0, MiniMax and Pixverse 6 (C, one review); Seedance 2.5 on Magnific not found.

**Price (USD, tax not stated).** From Magnific's own pricing page via snippet: Premium US$20 monthly or US$14.50/mo billed annually, 240,000 credits a year; Premium+ US$45 or US$33.75/mo annually, 600,000 credits a year; Pro Starter US$110 or US$82.50/mo annually (A via snippet). Credits on these plans are valid for a year and do not reset monthly (A via snippet). Whether a monthly payer gets the year's pool up front or a twelfth each month: UNSETTLED. Older third-party pages give Pro as US$280/US$210 with 4M credits (C, likely superseded). Euro prices also appear, so pricing may be regional (C).
- What credits buy, from Magnific's own tables: Nano Banana Pro 75 credits per 1K or 2K image, 150 at 4K (one listing says 250 at 4K); Kling 3.0 70, 90 or 400 credits a second at 720p, 1080p, 4K; Veo 3.1 200 a second at 720p and 1080p, Veo 3.1 Fast 100, Lite 40 (A via snippet, Magnific docs). So Premium's 240,000 credits is about 3,200 Nano Banana Pro images a year, or about 2,600 seconds of Kling 3.0 at 1080p a year, or about 1,200 seconds of Veo 3.1 a year. A third-party guide gives 25 credits per Nano Banana Pro image instead of 75 (C, conflict noted).
- "Unlimited." Premium+ and up include unlimited use of a short list of models: Nano Banana 2 and Kling 2.5 named on the pricing page (A via snippet); a reviewer counts about 30 unlimited models including Seedream 5.0 Pro, Flux.2 Pro and Nano Banana Pro at 1K and 2K, another counts about 10 (C, conflicting). One check dated 7 Oct 2026 says Nano Banana Pro is unlimited only on Pro (C). UNSETTLED; read it on the page on the day. Video and upscaling always draw credits (C).
- AUD estimate: Premium about A$34/mo monthly with GST; Premium+ about A$77/mo monthly with GST, or about A$58/mo on annual billing paid up front.

**Free tier.** Up to about 20 AI images a day on the in-house model and 10 stock downloads, personal use only, attribution required, no commercial licence (C, several; one notes Magnific's own pages say only "limited"). Fine for trying it, not for anything published.

**Commercial use.** Paid plans include a commercial licence for stock and AI output with no attribution (C, several; Magnific statement quoted). Note that a hub's licence and the underlying model maker's terms are two layers; no source settled whether, for example, Kling's own usage-right limits pass through (UNSETTLED).

**Claude.** Yes, official. Magnific's MCP docs: add `https://mcp.magnific.com`, sign in once with your Magnific account by OAuth, works in Claude's web app and Claude Code, and draws on the same credit balance as the web app; any Magnific account can connect (A via snippet, docs.magnific.com/modelcontextprotocol). VentureBeat confirms Magnific One is reachable from Claude and ChatGPT through it (B).

**Ease of use.** The broadest "everything in one place" of the four: stock photos, templates, AI images, video, upscaling. For an owner who already downloads Freepik stock, it is the same login.

**Red flags.** Trustpilot complaints on both freepik.com and magnific.com: declined charges three to six times the plan price, unused credits removed on cancellation or renewal, a banned annual account with no refund, a £150 charge while cancelling an £8 plan (C, individual reviews, 2025 to mid-2026). Magnific replies publicly and asks for tickets. Annual credit pools plus "credits vanish on cancel" is the risk to name.

**Proposed verdict.** Only if the owner already uses Freepik stock or wants stock, AI images and Brand Kit on one bill. Start monthly on Premium; do not buy annual until a month of use shows the credit burn.

Sources: magnific.com/pricing, magnific.com/ai/docs/image-ai-models, magnific.com/ai/docs/ai-video-generator-credits, docs.magnific.com/modelcontextprotocol, magnific.com/one (A via snippet, seen 11 Oct); prnewswire.com Freepik becomes Magnific (A, Apr 2026) and Magnific One release (A, 8 Oct 2026); thenextweb.com/news/freepik-rebrands-as-magnific (B, Apr 2026); venturebeat.com Magnific One (B, 8 Oct 2026); unite.ai Magnific One (C, Oct 2026); eesel.ai, autokeyworder.com (checked 7 Oct 2026), pikes.ai, renderahouse.com, aiunpacking.com, photutorial.com, promoclock.co (C, 2026); x.com/techhalla sponsored test (C, 6 Oct 2026); trustpilot.com freepik.com and magnific.com regional pages (C, 2025 to 2026).

### 1.3 Adobe Firefly

**What it does, plainly.** Adobe's AI image and video app, which since 2025 also carries other makers' models ("partner models") next to Adobe's own. One Adobe login and one credit balance.

**Models now.** Adobe's own Firefly image and video models, plus partner models: Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1) and Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2, FLUX.1.1 Pro, FLUX Kontext and FLUX.2, Runway Gen-4 and Gen-4.5, Luma Ray2 Flash, Veo 3.1 (A via snippet, helpx partner-models page and Adobe's Nano Banana page, seen 11 Oct); Kling 3.0 and Kling 3.0 Omni added in April 2026 to a roster Adobe calls "more than 30" models (A via snippet, Adobe newsroom, Apr 2026). Seedance is named in a restriction note (C). Not found: GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2.1, Seedance 2.5, Ideogram. Adobe limits Kling and Seedance to individual plans and keeps China-origin partner models off enterprise plans (C, single source).

**Price (USD).** Standard US$9.99/mo, 2,000 credits; Pro US$19.99/mo, 4,000 credits, with Adobe Express Premium and Photoshop on web and mobile; Pro Plus US$49.99/mo, 10,000 credits; Premium US$199.99/mo, 50,000 credits (C, several agree, Aug to Sep 2026). First-year discounts on Pro Plus and Premium reportedly ended 26 Aug 2026 (C). No AUD price found; check adobe.com/au.
- What credits buy, from Adobe's help page: Nano Banana 2 20 credits per image at 1K or 2K, GPT Image 2 5 to 80 by quality, FLUX.2 20, Veo 3.1 50 credits a second, Runway Gen-4.5 40 a second at 720p, several marked "limited time" (A via snippet, helpx). So Pro's 4,000 credits is about 200 Nano Banana 2 images, or about 80 seconds of Veo 3.1. Plans above Standard add "unlimited" on selected models and resolutions, which Adobe runs as rolling time-limited offers (A via snippet, helpx promotions page; B, 9to5Mac, Feb 2026). What is unlimited this month: UNSETTLED.
- AUD estimate: Pro about A$34/mo with GST at the convention rate.

**Free tier.** A small daily allowance on a curated set of models; Adobe does not publish the number (C, conflicting).

**Commercial use.** The strongest of any hub for Adobe's own models: Adobe sells its Firefly models as trained on licensed content and "commercially safe", and Content Credentials are attached to every output (B, TechSpot; A, Adobe). Partner models are different: an Adobe Community answer says partner-model output "may not be" commercially safe, and the partner makers have not made Adobe's training commitments (B, Yahoo Tech; C, community thread). Whether Adobe's IP indemnity excludes partner-model output in so many words: UNSETTLED (the enterprise wording found says "most Firefly-powered workflows").

**Claude.** Yes, official. Adobe launched the "Adobe for creativity" connector for Claude on 28 April 2026, installed from Claude's connector directory, giving Claude about 50 tools across Photoshop, Illustrator, Firefly, Express, Premiere, Lightroom, InDesign and Stock (A, Adobe blog; B, PetaPixel and No Film School). Whether it reaches partner models or only Adobe's own Firefly model: UNSETTLED. Free versus signed-in tool counts come from one blog only (C).

**Ease of use.** The most familiar brand to an owner, and the same login as Adobe Express if they already use it. The partner models sit in one menu.

**Red flags.** Credit costs marked "limited time" change often, and unlimited offers come and go. Adobe's subscription cancellation fees on annual plans are a known complaint category (not researched here; check before an annual plan).

**Proposed verdict.** Only if the owner already pays for Adobe (Express or Photoshop) or wants the most commercially cautious option; then Firefly Pro is the cheapest way to reach Nano Banana, GPT Image, Veo and Kling on one bill. Use Adobe's own model when the image must be safe to sell or print.

Sources: helpx.adobe.com partner models, generative credits FAQ, current Firefly promotions (A via snippet, seen 11 Oct, page ages about 2 to 60 days); adobe.com Firefly Nano Banana page (A via snippet); news.adobe.com April 2026 creative agent release (A, Apr 2026); blog.adobe.com "Adobe for creativity" connector (A, 28 Apr 2026); petapixel.com and nofilmschool.com (B, 28 Apr 2026); 9to5mac.com unlimited generations (B, 2 Feb 2026); tech.yahoo.com and techspot.com on partner-model safety (B, 2025 to 2026); community.adobe.com (C); saascrmreview.com, aitoolanalysis.com, photutorial.com, future-stack-reviews.com (C, 2026).

### 1.4 OpenArt

**What it does, plainly.** A model aggregator in the browser: one library, many makers' image and video models, plus character, audio and workflow helpers so you can run the same prompt on several models.

**Models now.** Video: Veo 3.1, Kling Omni and Kling 3 Omni, Seedance 2.5 and 2.0, Wan 3.0, MiniMax H3 (C, one review; OpenArt's own blogs name Seedance 2.5 and Kling 3 Omni). Image: GPT Image 2, Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, Seedream 5.0 Pro (A via snippet, OpenArt MCP page).

**Price (USD).** Restructured in 2026 (C, several). Starter US$14/mo, 4,000 credits; Plus US$34/mo, 12,000; Pro US$56/mo, 24,000; Wonder US$240/mo, 106,000 (C, most sources agree on monthly). Annual discounts reported as 10% to 27% depending on tier (C, conflicting). Extra credits about US$15 for 5,000 on Plus and up (C, single source).
- What credits buy: no official credit table found. Third-party estimates: Starter about 4,000 basic images but only two or three premium video clips; a 5-second Kling clip about 100 or 500 credits depending on source and plan generation (C, conflicting). UNSETTLED.
- AUD estimate: Plus (the first tier with commercial rights) about A$58/mo with GST.

**Free tier.** A trial only: 40 one-time credits that expire after 7 days, roughly 40 images and no video (C, several agree).

**Commercial use.** Starts at Plus. Starter is non-commercial (C, several cite OpenArt's help centre). This matters: the cheapest plan does not cover business use.

**Claude.** Yes, official. Custom connector at `https://mcp.openart.ai/mcp`, OAuth sign-in with an OpenArt account, spends the same credits as the website; on Team or Enterprise an admin adds it once (A via snippet, openart.ai/mcp, seen 11 Oct).

**Ease of use.** Comparison sites describe it as built for model-hopping, which suits a curious creator more than a busy owner (C).

**Red flags.** Trustpilot about 3.4 to 3.5 from about 510 reviews (C). Complaints: charged after cancelling under an unpublished "strict cancellation policy", billing continuing after a downgrade to Free, refund refusals; others report same-day refunds (C, Jan to May 2026). Prices rose this year (C).

**Proposed verdict.** Skip. Its cheapest plan has no commercial licence, it costs more than Krea for the same kind of access, and it adds nothing an owner needs that Krea, Magnific or Higgsfield does not.

Sources: openart.ai/mcp and openart.ai/blog pages (A via snippet, seen 11 Oct; vendor blogs also count as marketing); aireiter.com OpenArt review 2026 (C), promptsrush.com, affmaven.com, sloptv.co, theexpertranking.com, checkthat.ai, imgtoimg-ai.com (C, 2026); trustpilot.com openart.ai regional pages (C, late 2025 to May 2026).

## 2. Fal.ai for a non-technical owner

File 03 called fal "built for developers" and proposed Skip for most. What a non-developer actually meets today is friendlier than that, though it is still not a consumer app.

**There are three ways in without code.**
1. **Sandbox** (fal.ai/sandbox). Type one prompt, pick several models, run them side by side, see a colour-coded cost estimate before you press go (green means under US$1), and search past results by text, similar image or colour (A via snippet, fal Sandbox docs). The Sandbox page itself says "15 free generations today. No account needed" (A via snippet, seen 11 Oct). File 03 recorded free Sandbox credits on some models marked with a gift icon; this is consistent with it, and the exact daily free allowance per model is UNSETTLED. Video in the browser needs sign-in and is charged per second (A via snippet, fal tools page).
2. **Playground.** One model per page with a form: prompt box, options, Run. It also shows the code, which an owner ignores (A via snippet, fal Playground docs). A reviewer calls it beginner-friendly for testing but says "if you want a no-code interface, look elsewhere" (C).
3. **fal Agent.** A chat interface, launched 12 August 2026 in early access, that plans and runs multi-step image, video and 3D jobs across fal's models. On by default for any signed-in account; no plan or contract needed; fal charges only for the model runs, and the agent's reasoning, web search and video understanding are free "for now" (A via snippet, fal Agent access-and-pricing docs). Optional monthly credit plans: Starter US$50 for US$50 of credit, Pro US$200 with 5% off, Max US$1,000 with 10% off; unused credit rolls over (A via snippet). Unite.ai reported add-ons "starting at US$200"; fal's own docs are more current (conflict noted). For an owner, fal Agent is the closest thing fal has to "tell it what you want".

**Sign-up and topping up.** Sign in, then buy prepaid credit in the billing dashboard. If the balance falls below the account's lock threshold, the account locks and requests are refused until you top up. Purchased credit expires 365 days after purchase (A via snippet, fal FAQ). A minimum top-up is not published (C, two pages say so); UNSETTLED. A sign-up credit is not published either; sources range from none to "free credits and coupons" (C, conflicting). New accounts can run two jobs at once, rising automatically to 40 as more credit is bought (A via snippet, fal concurrency docs). There is no monthly plan unless you choose fal Agent's optional ones.

**What a typical small job costs (USD, from fal's own pages unless marked).**
- Ten Nano Banana 2.1 images at 1K: about US$0.40 (US$0.040 each; US$0.059 at 2K, US$0.134 at 4K; billed on tokens used) (A via snippet, fal Nano Banana 2.1 page, about 2 days old). Google's own API price is lower, about US$0.034 (B/C, Oct 2026).
- One Nano Banana Pro image: about US$0.15, double at 4K (A via snippet, an older fal page about 325 days old).
- An 8-second Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash clip at 720p: about US$0.80 (US$0.10 a second) (A via snippet, fal video guide; C, TeamDay agrees).
- An 8-second Veo 3.1 clip at 1080p with sound: about US$3.20 (file 03, C).
- A 5-second Kling 3.0 Pro clip at 1080p: about US$0.56 silent, US$0.84 with sound (A via snippet, fal Kling page, about six months old).
- A 10-second Seedance 2.5 clip at 720p: about US$4.73 (US$0.473 a second) per fal's own guide; third-party rates for the same model range from about US$0.20 to US$0.57 a second (A via snippet, conflicting C). UNSETTLED.
- So a month of ten images and four short clips is roughly US$5 to US$20 (about A$9 to A$34 with GST), far less than any hub subscription, if the owner can handle choosing a model page.

**Claude connector, settled.** fal's current Claude page gives the custom connector route: in Claude, Customize, Connectors, Add custom connector, name it fal, URL `https://mcp.fal.ai/mcp-relay`, then sign in through the browser by OAuth; no API key. On Team or Enterprise a workspace owner adds it first. The MCP server is free; only model runs are charged (A via snippet, fal.ai/docs/documentation/setting-up/mcp/claude, seen 11 Oct). The older key-based URL `https://mcp.fal.ai/mcp` with a bearer token is the Claude Code command route, not the app connector, which explains the conflict file 03 recorded. fal's Claude Code page now also uses the relay URL with browser sign-in (A via snippet). The "custom connectors not yet supported" page is the stale one.

**Commercial use, partly settled.** Each model on fal carries its own licence; most are marked with a "Commercial use" badge on the model page, and models marked "Research only" are non-commercial (A via snippet, fal FAQ). fal claims no rights in outputs, gives no warranty that outputs are original or non-infringing, says outputs may not be unique, and offers no indemnity (A via snippet, fal Terms of Service updated 3 March 2026). So: check the badge on each model page; the risk is the owner's.

**Other fal products an owner may meet.** fal Recast (announced 1 October 2026): swap a character in a 5 to 30 second video, billed per second (A, fal on X; C for the limits). fal's free browser image tools and the five free MiniMax H3 Max video generations a day (file 03).

**Proposed verdict (revised from file 03).** Only if the owner wants to pay per picture rather than a subscription, or wants Claude to make images and video through a connector, and will spend twenty minutes learning the Sandbox. Load US$10 to US$20 to start. For an owner who will never open a model page, Krea or Higgsfield is the friendlier front door to the same models.

Sources: fal.ai/sandbox and fal.ai/docs/documentation/model-apis/sandbox, playground, faq, concurrency-limits pages (A via snippet, seen 11 Oct); fal.ai/docs/documentation/agent/access-and-pricing (A via snippet); fal.ai/docs/documentation/setting-up/mcp/claude and claude-code (A via snippet); blog.fal.ai MCP server post (A via snippet, undated); fal.ai/models/google/nano-banana-2.1 (A via snippet, about 9 Oct 2026); fal.ai/learn/tools/ai-video-generators and seedance-2-0-vs-kling-3-0 (A via snippet, vendor guides); fal.ai/legal/terms-of-service (A via snippet, 3 Mar 2026); unite.ai fal Agent (C, Aug 2026); aireiter.com, costbench.com, vibecoding.gallery (C, Oct 2026); teamday.ai, rizzgen.ai (C, Sep 2026); explainx.ai, felloai.com on Nano Banana 2.1 pricing (C, Oct 2026); techpilot.ai on fal Recast (C, Oct 2026).

## 3. Midjourney

**What it does.** Makes pictures, with a strong house look, from a prompt on midjourney.com (Discord is no longer the only way in). Also animates its own stills into short silent clips.

**Current version.** V8.2 has been the default since 24 July 2026, "focused on aesthetics, image quality, and Personalization" (A via snippet, docs.midjourney.com Version page). V8.1 released 14 April 2026 per the same page (A via snippet); one source gives 30 April (C). The first V8 alpha was 17 March 2026, with native 2K (C). An Edit Model opened to everyone on 27 August 2026 and replaces Omni Reference, Character Reference and Retexture (A via snippet for the replacement; C for the date). Reported gains: about five times faster than V7, native 2K, better text and prompt understanding (C).

**Video.** Video V1, released 19 June 2025: animates a Midjourney image into a 5-second clip, extendable to about 20 or 21 seconds, silent, 480p on Basic and 720p on higher plans in Fast mode (C, several). One source says V1 shipped in April 2026; the June 2025 date is the consistent one (C, conflict noted). No newer video model, no sound, and no HD video found as of 11 Oct; one reviewer says the video tool "has had no updates in a long time" (C). James's experience with it is current: it has not changed.

**Plans (USD, tax not stated).** Basic US$10/mo, Standard US$30, Pro US$60, Mega US$120; about 20% off annually (US$8, US$24, US$48, US$96) (C, several agree; the official page was not reached). Fast GPU time about 3.3, 15, 30 and 60 hours; extra Fast time about US$4 an hour (C). Relax mode (unlimited, slower): none on Basic, unlimited images from Standard, and Relax video on Pro and Mega (C). Stealth mode (images hidden from the public gallery) from Pro (C). AUD estimate: Standard about A$51/mo with GST.

**Free tier.** None usable. Where a trial exists, output is under a non-commercial licence (C, single source).

**Commercial rights.** Paid subscribers own their output, with one condition: a company, or an employee of a company, with more than US$1 million a year in gross revenue must be on Pro or Mega to own its assets (C, several quoting the Terms; versions dated 12 Feb and 27 May 2026 cited). Most of James's clients are under US$1 million, but a busy builder or furniture retailer may not be; ask. Public gallery: on Basic and Standard every image is visible on midjourney.com, which matters for an unreleased product (C, inferred from Stealth being a Pro feature).

**Legal history.** Disney and Universal sued Midjourney for copyright infringement in June 2025; Warner Bros. Discovery's suit was consolidated in November 2025; the court ordered mediation to finish by 19 August 2026 with a status conference on 31 August; no outcome found (B, Mumbrella for the original suit; C, GraphicPolicy for the court dates). Same rule as Seedance: never prompt a character, celebrity or brand.

**API or Claude route.** No official API, waitlist or developer programme as of August 2026; an enterprise API was floated in a survey only (C, several). No official MCP server. Third-party wrappers exist, including a hosted connector from AceDataCloud that adds to Claude by OAuth, but they drive Midjourney accounts in ways one source says breach Midjourney's automation and resale terms (C). Do not recommend any of them; the risk is a banned account. The working route is Claude writes the prompt, the owner pastes it into midjourney.com.

**Not on the public leaderboards.** Midjourney does not appear on the Artificial Analysis or arena.ai image boards checked (section 4), presumably because there is no API to test it through. So there is no blind-vote ranking to compare it with GPT Image 2.5 or Nano Banana; only reviews.

**Best at.** A distinctive, art-directed look: mood, illustration, stylised product scenes, brand imagery that does not look like stock. Personalization and moodboards learn your taste. Not the pick for words on a picture, for a faithful photo of a real product, for video with sound, or for anything driven from Claude.

**Proposed verdict.** Only if the owner wants a distinctive style (a café's illustrated menu boards, a tour operator's poster look) and will use the website. Standard (US$30, about A$51 with GST) for unlimited relaxed images; Pro if the business turns over more than US$1 million or needs images kept private. Skip it for video.

### 4.4 What this means for an owner, in one paragraph

The top image model (GPT Image 2.5) is cheapest to reach through ChatGPT, and the top video model (Gemini Omni) through a Google AI plan, which is the conclusion of file 03 and the leaderboards confirm it. A hub earns its place only when the owner wants the models in between: Seedance for a 30-second product clip, Kling for value, Wan for cheap long clips, Seedream for multilingual signage, Recraft for a logo. The hubs are mostly a month behind the model makers on version numbers.

## 5. Proposed verdicts for the hubs (James makes the final call)

| Hub | Proposed verdict | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Fal.ai | Only if the owner wants pay-per-picture or Claude to drive it, and will learn the Sandbox | Cheapest per result, every major model, official Claude connector with browser sign-in; still a developer site in feel |
| Higgsfield | Only if video or ad creative is a weekly job, monthly billing (unchanged from file 03) | Widest presets and an official Claude connector; billing complaints and unclear pricing |
| Krea | Only if the owner wants one cheap login across many models | Basic about A$15/mo is the cheapest commercial licence; video needs Pro (about A$60); strict refund rule |
| Magnific (Freepik) | Only if the owner already uses Freepik stock or wants Brand Kit, monthly billing | Stock, AI and an official Claude connector on one bill; annual credit pools and cancellation complaints |
| Adobe Firefly | Only if the owner already pays for Adobe, or wants the most commercially cautious option | Adobe's own model is sold as commercially safe and the Claude connector is official; partner-model output is not covered the same way |
| OpenArt | Skip | Cheapest plan has no commercial licence, dearer than Krea, nothing extra for this reader |
| Midjourney (not a hub) | Only if a distinctive style is the point; Skip for video | Best look, no Claude route, silent video unchanged since 2025, US$1M revenue rule |
| Leonardo, Flora, Artlist | Skip | Reached better through Canva; built for design teams; built for video editors |

## Open questions this research could not settle

- Every hub's credit-to-output conversion for video: Krea's Veo 3.1 and Kling cost in units, OpenArt's credit table, Firefly's current "unlimited" list, Magnific's unlimited list (counts range from 2 to 30 models).
- Whether Magnific's monthly payers get the 240,000 or 600,000 annual credit pool up front or monthly.
- Whether the Adobe for creativity connector reaches partner models or only Adobe's own Firefly model; whether Adobe's indemnity explicitly excludes partner-model output.
- Whether a free Krea, Magnific or OpenArt account can generate through its Claude connector.
- fal: minimum top-up, any sign-up credit, and the per-model daily free Sandbox allowance.
- Whether hub commercial licences override the underlying model makers' own terms (for example Kling's usage right).
- Seedance 2.5 on Magnific and Firefly; GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2.1 on Krea, Firefly and OpenArt.
- Kling 4.0 full launch date (due this month); Utopai X public availability.
- Midjourney's official plan page (not reached; all plan figures are third-party) and the Disney and Universal mediation outcome.
- AUD prices for every hub; none publishes one in the sources found.
