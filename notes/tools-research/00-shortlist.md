11 October 2026. Built from files 01 to 04 in this folder, which hold every source and grade. Each verdict below is a proposal; nothing is built until James approves the set. Prices are as the vendor states them, in the vendor's currency, and will be written loosely on the page ("about US$20 a month").

One conflict settled here: Google AI Plus is A$7.99 a month (file 01, cut in June 2026), not A$12.99 (file 03, a single January source quoting the pre-cut price). File 02 of the social research carries the old figure too.

Shape of the page: grouped by job, not by vendor, because the tools nest. Gemini already covers images and video, ChatGPT covers images, Claude covers slides. Every job opens with "check what you already pay for", which is where the recommender earns its keep.

## Where the proposal differs from the research

- **Wispr Flow.** The research said Only if (you dictate most of the day). Proposed Worth it with "start free" instead, because James uses it daily and teaches it, and the free weekly cap is the natural test of whether someone needs it.
- **Granola.** The research said Only if. Proposed Skip for now, because recommending a tool under a live consent lawsuit, when the tool James uses does the same job, is a cost with no upside. Revisit when the case moves.
- **Gemini and ChatGPT as image and video tools** are cards under their jobs as well as assistants, so the recommender can say "you already have this".

## Facts that would make a recommendation wrong if missed

- OpenAI closed Sora in April and its API on 24 Sep. ChatGPT makes no video now.
- Free Gemini accounts dropped to Flash-Lite on 9 Oct. Gemini 4 Argon is restricted to security partners.
- Claude Docs, Slides and Design left beta on 8 Oct on every plan, switched on by default from 15 Oct.
- ChatGPT Free and Go moved to GPT-6 Luna on 8 Oct; ads show in Australia.
- Manus deleted some users' data in August after its Meta deal unwound, then raised over US$500M on 8 Oct.
- Buffer is not in Claude's connector directory (Metricool and Postiz are); the social deck already treats it as a custom connector.
