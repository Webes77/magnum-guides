# 07 · Six jobs the tools page does not cover yet, October 2026

Researched 14 October 2026 by web search only, to widen "Which AI Tools Are Worth Paying For?" beyond the jobs in files 01 to 05. The reader is an Australian owner with 1 to 10 staff (tradies, cafés, retailers, tour operators, consultants) who already dabbles with AI. Six jobs: answering the phone, customer chat, email, websites, voice and video presenters, and the AI already inside software the business pays for. No vendor page was fetched; every figure comes from search-result snippets, mostly in WebSearch extended mode. Where a snippet quotes the vendor's own page it is graded A with "via snippet". Vendor pricing pages for Rosie, Goodcall, Smith.ai, Retell, Bland, Fyxer, Superhuman, Wix, Squarespace, Framer, Lovable, Durable, ElevenLabs, HeyGen, Synthesia, CapCut, Descript, Opus Clip, Canva, Xero and MYOB never appeared directly in results, so most prices below are C grade and say so. claude.com, anthropic.com, support.claude.com and openai.com are blocked from the sandbox per the standing note; one claude.com marketplace page surfaced as a snippet and is graded A.

Grades: A = vendor's own current page (here always read via search snippet, never fetched). B = reputable press. C = blog, reseller, comparison site, forum. Most of the AI receptionist market is written up by AI receptionist vendors comparing themselves with competitors; those pages are C and flagged as vendor-written.

Currency convention: every price says AUD or USD and whether tax is in, where the source says. AUD is given wherever a vendor lists AUD. Where an AUD equivalent is estimated it assumes about A$1.55 per US$1, matching file 01. It is an estimate, not a quote. Never invented: a figure no search could settle reads UNSETTLED.

These tools answer when you don't, take the details, and either book the job or text you a summary. The usual setup is a divert on no answer from your existing mobile or landline to the AI's number, so you keep your number.

### Australian law for this job

- **Call recording.** State laws differ. Queensland lets a party to a call record it without the other side's consent but restricts sharing; NSW generally requires consent; sources disagree on Victoria. Where caller and business are in different states the conservative course is all-party consent. Every receptionist above records and transcribes, so the practical rule is: the AI says the call is recorded in its first sentence. Sources: sprintlaw.com.au "Business Call Recording Laws in Australia (2026)" (C); astorlegal.com.au (C); mondaq.com (C). Not legal advice; the page should say "tell callers they are recorded" and no more.
- **Automated decisions.** From 10 December 2026, APP 1.7 requires covered entities to say in their privacy policy when a computer program makes or substantially helps make a decision that significantly affects someone. Covered means mainly businesses over A$3M turnover, so most readers are outside it, but an owner should check. Sources: gtlaw.com.au (B, law firm); corrs.com.au (B, law firm); hamiltonlocke.com.au (B, law firm).
- **Outbound calls** (a bot ringing customers) bring in the Do Not Call Register and telemarketing rules. Nothing here should be set up to make outbound sales calls. Not researched further.

### Changed in the last 90 days

- No carrier product: no Telstra or Optus AI receptionist launch found; vendors connect to Telstra and Optus business lines by call divert. open.cx (C, vendor); trillet.ai (C, vendor).
- ServiceM8 Phone Agent setup guides dated August 2026 indicate it is now on the A$199 Unlimited plan. webblend.au (C, 27 Aug 2026).

### Job verdict

Real demand and the clearest money case on the page (one missed job a month pays for it), but the weakest evidence: almost every comparison is written by a vendor. **Pick: ServiceM8 Phone Agent if you are on ServiceM8; otherwise trial Hey Jodie for a week on a missed-call divert.** The page should tell the owner to test with five calls of their own before trusting it, and to make it announce recording.
