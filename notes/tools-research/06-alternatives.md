# 06 · The alternatives an owner will ask about, October 2026

Researched 14 October 2026 by web search only (WebSearch, extended mode for every price and anything from the last three months), to widen "The AI Tools Worth Paying For" so a well-informed owner finds the names they have heard of, with a plain verdict on each. The reader is an Australian small business owner with 1 to 10 staff who already dabbles with AI. Files 00 to 05 in this folder were read first and nothing they cover is redone here: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Manus, Claude Slides, Gamma, Canva (as a deck tool), Wispr Flow, Fireflies, Granola, the Zoom, Meet and Teams notetakers, the picture and video tools, Buffer, Meta Business Suite, Zapier, Make and n8n. Superwhisper was already given Skip in file 02 and is only re-checked here.

No vendor page was fetched directly. Under the standing note, claude.com, anthropic.com, openai.com and microsoft.com are blocked, and the earlier files found most other vendor hosts fail from the sandbox, so every figure below comes from search-result snippets. Where a snippet quotes the vendor's own page it is graded A with "via snippet". Most pricing came through comparison sites, many of them written by a competing product, and that is said where it applies.

Grades: A = vendor's own current page or help centre (here always via search snippet). B = reputable press. C = blog, review or comparison site, reseller, forum, aggregator. Dates are the source's own date where shown, otherwise "undated"; "Oct 2026" means a page that says it was checked in October.

Currency convention: every price says AUD or USD (or EUR) and whether tax is in, as the vendor states it. USD prices are what the vendor lists, usually ex tax. A USD tool billed to an Australian card normally adds 10% GST at checkout and the bank may add a foreign transaction fee of up to about 3%. No AUD conversion is given unless the vendor lists AUD. A figure no search could settle is written UNSETTLED.

The verdict ladder is the page's: **Top pick**, **Good if** (with the condition in a few words), or **Skip**, each with a one-line reason. Every verdict is a proposal for James.
