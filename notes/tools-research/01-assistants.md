# 01 · Chat assistants and do-it-for-you agents, October 2026

Researched 11 October 2026 by web search only, for a recommendations page aimed at an Australian small business owner with 1 to 10 staff who already dabbles with AI. Every direct fetch failed from the sandbox: gemini.google, blog.google, workspaceupdates.googleblog.com and petboost.com.au all returned DNS failures, and claude.com, anthropic.com, support.claude.com, openai.com, microsoft.com and manus.im are blocked per the standing note. So every figure below comes from search-result snippets. Where a snippet quotes the vendor's own page, it is graded A with "via snippet" noted. Leads from James's Sunday Brief digests (27 Sep to 10 Oct) were each checked by search and are graded on what the search found, not on the newsletter.

Grades: A = vendor's own current page (here always read via search snippet, never fetched). B = reputable press. C = blog, reseller, comparison site, forum.

Currency convention: every price says AUD or USD and whether tax is in. AUD is given wherever the vendor lists AUD. USD tools billed to an Australian card usually add 10% GST at checkout, plus a possible foreign transaction fee of up to about 3%. Where an AUD equivalent is estimated it assumes about A$1.55 per US$1, matching `notes/social-research/02-tools.md`. It is an estimate, not a quote.

Compared with `02-tools.md` (23 Sep 2026), three things moved. Google AI Plus is A$7.99, not A$12.99 (cut in June, missed last time). The "A$149.99" snippet that file flagged against Google AI Pro is the entry Google AI Ultra price on the same page, so AI Pro is A$32.99 and the flag is resolved. And the Claude Free Projects question is answered: up to five Projects on Free, per third-party checks.

## Category verdict

For a 1 to 10 person Australian business, the honest default is **one paid assistant, chosen by where the business already works**, plus free tiers beside it.

- **Claude Pro (A$34 incl GST)** is the default paid tool for an owner whose work is words: quotes, emails, proposals, policies. It now reaches Google Docs, Sheets and Slides and Microsoft 365, so it no longer forces a choice of office suite.
- **Gemini** is the first thing a Google Workspace business should switch on, because on Business Standard and up it is already paid for. Do not stack a consumer Google AI plan on top. For images and video it is the strongest of the three.
- **Copilot** is the same logic for a Microsoft 365 business: use the free Copilot Chat first. The paid add-on (AU$31.40 ex GST per user, yearly) is a per-person cost that only pays for itself when staff live in Outlook and Excel all day.
- **ChatGPT** is the pick when the owner will pay for only one tool and needs pictures as well as words. Its free tier, now on GPT-6 Luna, is the best free option of the five for occasional use, with ads.
- **Manus** sits in a different slot: not a daily assistant but a contractor for whole jobs. Try it free; pay only once a job has earned it.

Flag under hard rule 7: two products here bill on usage that a small business cannot predict (Manus credits, Microsoft's new Copilot Credits), and ChatGPT's agent features (Dots) start at US$100 a month. None belongs in a default stack.
