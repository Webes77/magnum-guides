## 3. Meeting notes and transcripts

### Consent, which applies to every tool below

All four vendors that address it put consent on the user: Fireflies' platform rules, Granola's documentation, Wispr's reviewers and OpenAI's Meetings help page all say so. In Australia, recording rules are state law and the sources disagree on how the states group. What they agree on: Queensland's Invasion of Privacy Act 1971 lets a party to a private conversation record it without the others' consent, and recording a conversation you are not part of is illegal everywhere. NSW, ACT, SA and WA are described as all-party consent by Sprintlaw, while Sonetel puts NSW, Tasmania and the ACT in a "only to protect lawful interests" group, and Fairwork supports WA as all-party. One-party consent covers making the recording, not sharing or publishing it. On a call across states, the safe course is the stricter rule. Practical line for the page: tell people the call is recorded, every time, whatever tool. Sources: sprintlaw.com.au (C, legal guidance blog, 2026); sonetel.com (C); fairworkmate.com.au (C, 2026); stonegroup.com.au (C). Not legal advice; no statute was read directly.

Bot-free tools (Granola, Wispr Notetaker, ChatGPT Meetings, Fireflies desktop "Take Notes") make this sharper, because nobody on the call sees anything join. **Chamberlain v. Granola**, a proposed US class action filed 30 Jul 2026, alleges exactly this under California's all-party law, plus model training on by default on Free and Business plans. Allegations only, no ruling. Source: computerworld.com (B, 2026); consentpixel.com and layer3labs.io (C).

## Operational complexity flags

1. **Credit systems** (Gamma, Fireflies AI credits, Canva Magic Studio): the cost and the limit both move month to month. Name the limit in the page rather than hiding it.
2. **Overlap is now the norm.** Fireflies does dictation, Wispr does meeting notes, Gamma connects to Granola, ChatGPT and Claude both do decks and notes. Recommend one tool per job and resist stacking.
3. **Bot-free recording** shifts consent entirely onto the owner and is under live litigation in the US. Teach the habit of saying it out loud.
4. **Admin switches.** Gemini notes and Teams recap both need an admin to turn them on; in a 1 to 10 person business that admin is usually the owner and nobody has told them.
