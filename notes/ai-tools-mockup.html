Your main AI

Hilux or Ranger?

Claude or ChatGPT is the same question. Both do the job. Pick on what you carry: Claude if your week is writing, quoting and long documents. ChatGPT if you want one app for words, pictures and voice. The mistake is paying for both before you’ve learnt one properly.

Top pick

Claude

Writing that sounds like a person, and long documents.

Price
A$34 a month, GST included
Free plan
Yes, for light use
Also does
Slides and docs, and works inside Google Docs and Microsoft 365
My take

It’s what I use every day. Put your business in a Project once and it stops needing the backstory. What it won’t do is make photos or video.

Top pick

ChatGPT

One app for words, pictures and voice.

Price
US$20 a month, about A$30 to A$35
Free plan
Yes, on GPT-6 since 8 October, with ads
Also does
The best pictures with words on them, and a voice mode that talks back
My take

I use it alongside Claude. If you’ll only pay for one tool and you want pictures as well as words, this is the one.

Good if you’re on Google Workspace

Gemini

Already inside Gmail, Docs and Meet, and the best at photos and video.

Price
In Workspace Business Standard. On its own, A$7.99 or A$32.99 a month
Free plan
Yes, on Google’s lightest model
My take

I use it for pictures and video. On Workspace, switch it on before you buy anything else.

Good if you’re on Microsoft 365

Copilot

Quick questions, inside the Microsoft account you already have.

Price
Copilot Chat is included. The paid version is AU$31.40 a person a month, plus GST
Free plan
Copilot Chat, with your Microsoft 365
My take

Use the free Copilot Chat first. The paid version costs as much per person as a whole Claude seat.
