Prompt 4 · One photo, every platform
1 photo · every platform
Where

A new chat inside your social media project. Attach the photo.

Then
  1. Fill the blank with one line: what it is, and anything worth knowing.
  2. Fix anything marked CHECK before you post.
  3. Copy each version to its platform, or send them to Buffer in step 4.
Time it the first time. Under two minutes is normal.
The promptTap a blank to fill it, then copy
Fill inExpandCopy
Role: You write social posts for this business, the way the owner would if they had the time.

Context: I have attached a photo. It shows what it is, and anything worth knowing, in one line. Use the voice file in this project for how we sound and what we must always include.

Constraints: Use only what is in the photo, my line above and the voice file. Never invent a customer, a result, a price, a date or a quote, because a made-up detail in a public post is the fastest way to lose trust. If a post needs a detail you do not have, write [CHECK] in its place. Mention one specific thing you can see in the photo, because a real detail is what makes a post sound like a person was there. Use at most one emoji and never a dash to join two thoughts, because both are the quickest signs a machine wrote it. Write each platform its own way rather than one post copied across, because each one is read differently.

Tone: As the voice file says.

Format: One labelled version for each platform listed in the voice file, and none for any we do not use. LINKEDIN: three to five lines on the work and what it shows about how we do things. INSTAGRAM: two to four short lines, then up to three hashtags.

03

What this commits to

Changes

What the new engine does

  • Sizes each slide to its content, so short slides get bigger type and no dead gap.
  • Four layouts: statement, rows, tiles and prompt, each with its own way of filling the frame.
  • The prompt card with Copy, Fill in and Expand in its header, and tap-to-fill blanks.
  • The phone view: the same slides as one scrolling page.
  • Navy cover with the badge on every deck.
Fixed

What does not move

  • No URL changes. Every link already sent keeps working.
  • Your logos are never redrawn or recoloured beyond the delivered files.
  • Prompts are never shrunk. The 15 pixel floor stays.
  • Every prompt stays byte-identical to its Prompt Shelf card.
  • No logins, no server, no tracking.
