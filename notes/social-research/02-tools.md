# 02 · Tool stack and cost for a beginner small business, late 2026

Researched 23 September 2026 by web search only. Almost every vendor pricing page was blocked from the sandbox when fetched directly (canva.com, buffer.com, later.com, metricool.com, publer.com, capcut.com, gemini.google, about.fb.com, linkedin.com all returned EGRESS_BLOCKED). So most figures below come from search-result snippets. Where a snippet quotes a vendor's own page, it is graded A with that caveat noted; otherwise B or C.

Grades: A = vendor's own current page (here usually read via search snippet, not fetched). B = reputable press. C = blog, reseller, comparison site, forum.

Currency convention: every price says AUD or USD and whether tax is in. USD tools billed to an Australian card will usually add 10% GST at checkout (most large US SaaS vendors are registered for Australian GST) plus a possible foreign transaction fee from the bank of up to about 3%. Where an AUD equivalent is given it assumes roughly A$1.55 per US$1 and is an estimate, not a quote. Check the rate on the day.

Treat every price here as moving. Five of the tools below changed pricing in 2026 alone (Canva, CapCut, Later, Metricool's X add-on, Meta One launch).

**Are avatars wise for a local business? Mostly no.** A local tradie, shop or sports club sells on "these are real people you can trust". An avatar presenter undercuts exactly that. Reports on avatar-hosted content say comment sections drift to discussing whether the host looks real rather than the product. Disclosure rules are tightening (Meta auto-labels content made with its own generative tools; New York's synthetic performer law took effect mid-2026). If used at all: explainer or FAQ videos only, clearly labelled as AI, never presented as the owner. Recommend the owner's own phone video plus Edits instead. ## Operational complexity flags

1. **Automation chains** (Zapier or Make linking Claude, Canva, a scheduler and a spreadsheet): each link is a thing that breaks silently, and a one-person business will not notice for weeks. Prefer one scheduler plus Claude scheduled tasks over multi-step Zaps. Permanent complexity.
2. **Per-channel and per-profile pricing** (Buffer, Publer, Meta One): the bill grows every time a platform is added. Decide channels first, tools second.
3. **Credit systems** (Opus Clip, HeyGen, Later AI credits, Firefly, Metricool add-ons): costs that vary month to month and expire. Avoid for beginners.
4. **Too many tools**: every extra login is a password, a renewal and a place brand assets drift out of sync. The standard stack is three paid-or-free tools, which is the ceiling to aim for.
5. **Avatar video**: a trust and disclosure liability for a local business, not an efficiency.
6. **CapCut free tier**: commercial-use terms for music and assets; plus some clients will object on ownership grounds.
7. **Meta One**: new, per-profile, likely to change. Revisit in six months.
8. **Canva scheduler gap**: it cannot schedule Instagram Reels, Stories or carousels. A course that teaches "schedule from Canva" will hit this in the first week. Teach Business Suite for Instagram.
